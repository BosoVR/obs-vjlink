# Sidewinda Flash Objects Visual Checklist

- Background reacts to kick/onset with sharp flash peaks.
- Flash decay is tunable from hardtechno punch to smoother pulse.
- Objects can be disabled without making the scene empty.
- Object modes include logo, primitive, and mesh placeholder.
- Strobe safety clamps brightness and flash rate.
- Works as a generator without input source.
- Works as an overlay/postprocess when input is present.
